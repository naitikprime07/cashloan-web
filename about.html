CashLoan is an online platform that helps you understand loans in simple words and choose the one that fits your needs and your budget.

What we do

Loans, explained simply

Choosing a loan can feel confusing. There are many types, and every one comes with its own amount, period, interest rate and fees. We explain personal, auto, student, business, payday, home and gold loans in plain language, so you can compare your options before you apply.

Our short step-by-step flow asks for your preferred repayment period, loan amount, type of work and loan type. It then shows you information that matches your choices. For each loan you can also check your eligibility and work out your EMI before you decide.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need

    Pick the repayment period, the amount, your work type and the loan type that suits you.

  2. Explore your options

    Read how each loan works, who can apply, the documents you need and the mistakes to avoid.

  3. Decide with confidence

    Compare costs, understand your EMI and take your time before you apply with a lender.
