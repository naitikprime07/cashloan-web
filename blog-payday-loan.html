What is a payday loan?

A payday loan is a small loan that a lender gives against your regular salary. You repay it in one payment or in a few short instalments, usually when your next salary arrives. Approval is quick and the paperwork is light, because the lender mainly checks that you have a steady salary.

That convenience has a price. The interest and fees on payday loans are much higher than on a personal loan, and the short period means the cost is charged over only a few weeks. Late fees can add up fast. Always check the total amount you will repay before you accept.

When it can make sense

A genuine emergency such as a medical bill or an urgent repair, when you are sure you can repay from your next salary.

When to avoid it

For shopping or trips, or to repay another loan. Taking a new payday loan to repay an old one can trap you in debt.

Who can apply

  • Salaried people with a regular salary paid into a bank account.
  • Age is usually 21 to 58.
  • A minimum monthly salary, often ₹15,000 to ₹25,000, depending on the lender.
  • A working phone number and a bank account in your own name, with valid KYC.

Documents you may need

  • Identity proof such as PAN, Aadhaar or passport.
  • Address proof.
  • The last 1 to 3 salary slips, or 3 months of bank statements that show your salary credit.
  • Employer details, such as the company name and your work email.
  • A selfie or video KYC, if the lender asks for it.

How to apply

  1. Ask if you really need it

    Check cheaper choices first, such as a salary advance from your employer, help from family or a small personal loan.

  2. Borrow the smallest amount

    Take only what you need until payday. A smaller loan costs less and is easier to repay.

  3. Check that the lender is genuine

    Choose a bank or an RBI-registered NBFC. Install loan apps only from the official app stores.

  4. Read the total cost

    Look at the key fact statement. It shows the interest, fees, taxes and the total you will repay.

  5. Apply and verify

    Enter your details, upload your documents and complete the KYC.

  6. Receive the money

    The loan should be paid straight into your own bank account.

  7. Repay on the due date

    Set a reminder or auto-debit so that you never pay late fees.

Smart tips before you borrow

  • Compare the total amount you repay, not only the daily or monthly rate.
  • Ask if there is a fee for early repayment. Repaying early can save interest.
  • Try a salary advance from your employer first. It is often free or much cheaper.
  • Keep even a small emergency fund, so that you do not need a payday loan again.
  • Never take a second loan to repay the first one.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Borrowing for things that are not urgent.
  • Ignoring late fees and rollover charges.
  • Taking loans from several apps at the same time.
  • Sharing your OTP or PIN, or giving apps access to your contacts and photos.

Common questions

Are payday loans safe?

They are legal when given by a bank or an RBI-registered NBFC. The risk is the high cost, so borrow only for a real emergency and only what you can repay.

What happens if I cannot repay on time?

The lender will charge late fees and interest, and may report the delay to credit bureaus, which can lower your credit score. Contact the lender early if you expect trouble.

Will a payday loan affect my credit score?

Yes. Repaying on time can help your record, while late or missed payments can hurt it.
