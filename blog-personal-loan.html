What is a personal loan?

A personal loan is unsecured. The lender decides how much to give based on your income, your existing debts and your credit score, not on an asset you own. Because the lender takes more risk, the interest rate is usually higher than for a home or auto loan.

Most lenders send the money straight to your bank account, often within a few days once your documents are checked. You then pay a fixed EMI every month until the loan ends. Many lenders also charge a processing fee, usually a small percentage of the loan amount.

Who can apply

  • Salaried people with a steady job, often with 6 months to 1 year at the current employer.
  • Self-employed people and professionals with regular income and 2 or more years in the business or practice.
  • Age is usually between 21 and 58 or 60, depending on the lender.
  • A credit score of 700 or more helps, and 750 or more often gets the best rates.

Documents you may need

  • Identity proof such as Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter ID.
  • Address proof such as a utility bill or rental agreement.
  • Income proof: the last 2 or 3 salary slips, or 6 months of bank statements. Self-employed people usually give income tax returns.
  • A recent passport-size photograph.
  • Bank account details where the money will be sent.

How to apply

  1. Decide how much you need

    Add up the exact cost of what you need and borrow only that, plus a small buffer for fees.

  2. Check your budget

    Use an EMI calculator. Try to keep all your EMIs together below about 40 to 50 percent of your monthly income.

  3. Check your credit score

    A higher score means better offers. Fix any errors in your credit report before you apply.

  4. Compare lenders

    Look at the interest rate, processing fee, prepayment charges and the total amount you will repay.

  5. Apply and upload documents

    Fill in the form and share your documents. Most lenders now do this online.

  6. Read the agreement

    Check the key fact statement. It shows the rate, fees and the total cost of the loan in one place.

  7. Receive the money and repay on time

    Set up auto-debit so that no EMI is missed.

Smart tips before you borrow

  • Compare the total cost of the loan, not just the EMI.
  • Choose the shortest tenure you can comfortably afford. You will pay less interest overall.
  • Ask whether part-payment or early closure is allowed and what it costs.
  • Avoid applying to many lenders at once. Each application can leave an enquiry on your credit report.
  • Set up auto-debit or a reminder a few days before every due date.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Borrowing more than you need just because you are eligible for it.
  • Ignoring the processing fee, GST and late-payment charges.
  • Using a personal loan for investing or speculation.
  • Signing without reading the terms and conditions.

Common questions

Can I get a personal loan with a low credit score?

It is harder. Some lenders may offer a smaller amount at a higher rate, or ask for a co-applicant. Improving your score first usually saves money.

Does a personal loan affect my credit score?

Paying on time helps you build a good score. Missed EMIs, or many new applications in a short time, can lower it.

How fast can I get the money?

Digital lenders can approve in minutes to a day for verified customers. Banks may take several days. It depends on your documents and the lender.
