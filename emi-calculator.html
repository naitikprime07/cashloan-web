How the EMI is worked out

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)
P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12), n = number of months

The figures are an illustration. The real EMI, rate and fees such as the processing fee depend on the lender.
