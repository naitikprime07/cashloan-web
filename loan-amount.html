Smart borrowing tips

  • Borrow only what you need. A bigger loan means a bigger EMI and more interest.
  • Include the processing fee and other charges in your calculation. They are often deducted from the amount you receive.
  • Make sure the EMI fits your monthly budget after essential expenses.
  • Keep your credit score healthy. It can help you qualify for a higher amount.

Common questions

Will I get exactly the amount I select?

Not always. The final amount depends on the lender's check of your income, repayment ability and credit profile, so it may differ from what you choose here.

Are there charges besides interest?

Usually there is a processing fee, plus taxes and possible late-payment or prepayment charges. Read the loan summary before you accept.

Does a bigger amount take longer to approve?

It can. Larger amounts may need more documents and verification.
