Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CashLoan (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our website. We keep things simple and collect as little as we need. By using the website you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Your selections. The repayment period, loan amount, work type and loan type you pick are kept only in your own browser for the current visit, so that we can show them back to you on the last step. They are not sent to our servers.
  • Calculator and eligibility details. The amounts and answers you enter in the EMI calculator and the eligibility check are used only in your browser to work out the result. They are not sent to our servers.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us through the form or by email, we receive your name, your email address and whatever you write in your message.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical details such as your IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time of the visit. This is used to run and protect the website.

How we use information

  • To show you the pages and information you ask for.
  • To reply to your questions and feedback.
  • To keep the website working properly, safe from misuse and to fix problems.
  • To follow the law when we are required to.

Cookies and similar technology

We use your browser’s session storage to remember your selections while you move between pages. It is cleared when you close the tab, and we do not use it to identify you. We use Google Ad Manager to display advertisements. On the loan blog list, we also use Meta Pixel to attempt one PageView event per browser profile on this site. A random first-party identifier and event marker are stored in IndexedDB to prevent repeat events across pages and visits. This marker persists until site storage is cleared or removed by the browser; it is not sent as a pixel parameter. These services may use cookies and receive page, browser and network information. Our PageView integration does not send your loan selections or form entries.

Third-party services

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, Google receives basic details such as your IP address and browser information. Our pages may also link to lenders and other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us run the website, and when the law requires it.

How long we keep information

Messages you send us are kept for as long as needed to reply and to meet legal requirements. Your selections stay in your browser only until you close the tab.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. No website or method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please do not send sensitive details such as OTPs, PINs, card numbers or passwords to us.

Your rights

Under applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. To make a request, use the details on our Contact page.

Children

This website is meant for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed. Please check it now and then.

If you have a question about this policy, please visit our Contact page.
